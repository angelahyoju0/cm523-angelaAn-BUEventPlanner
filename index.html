About This Project

Hi, fellow BU student leader!

Angela An
COM '26 · Media Science
Innovation & Entrepreneurship
LinkedIn

My name is Angela An—a fourth-year Media Science student at BU's College of Communication, with a minor in Innovation and Entrepreneurship at Questrom. I've spent my time here leading student organizations including the Asian Student Union and the legislative branch of Student Government, which means I've planned a lot of events.

And I know firsthand how confusing that process can be.

BU's official documentation on event planning, purchasing, contracting, and catering exemptions is dense, policy-heavy, and updated every year. For first-time organizers or newly elected e-board members, it's a lot to absorb on top of everything else.

This tool fixes that. Answer a few quick questions about your event, and it generates a personalized, step-by-step checklist showing only the steps that apply to your specific situation—no policy-reading required.
